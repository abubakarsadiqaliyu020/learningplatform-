FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some common questions about the EduLearn platform.

Who can use EduLearn?

Students, teachers and administrators can use the platform according to their assigned roles.

How do students access courses?

Students log in with credentials provided by the administrator and access their assigned courses.

Can teachers create assignments?

Yes. Teachers will be able to create modules, lessons and practical assignments for their courses.

Can students continue learning after submitting an assignment?

Yes. Assignment submission does not remove access to the module or lessons. Students can continue reading and practicing their course materials.
